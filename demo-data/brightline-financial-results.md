## Seasonality and year-over-year revenue growth

Year-over-year revenue growth in 2026: Q1 2026 +18.3% vs Q1 2025, Q2 2026 +18.1% vs Q2 2025, Q3 2026 +18.8% vs Q3 2025. Growth has been stable at about 18% per quarter.
